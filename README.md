# **Nexus Drift: Horizon Protocol** 🚀

Welcome to **Nexus Drift: Horizon Protocol** — a reimagined, performance-first PC gaming experience that transports you to a breathtaking orbital colony where gravity is optional and every decision reshapes the world around you. Born from the same creative lineage as the acclaimed Pragmata beta, this project pushes the boundaries of environmental storytelling, fluid traversal mechanics, and real-time adaptive difficulty.

Unlike traditional static game releases, **Nexus Drift** treats its beta as a living organism — continuously evolving through community feedback, telemetry-driven tuning, and modular content drops. Whether you're a seasoned speedrunner, a lore enthusiast, or a first-time explorer, this build offers a meticulously optimized foundation that breathes with your hardware, not against it.

## 🌌 Project Vision: A Universe That Learns Your Rhythm

Picture a world not crafted by level designers alone, but co-authored by every player who steps foot on the drifting station *Aurelia*. **Nexus Drift: Horizon Protocol** uses a proprietary "Adaptive Gravity Engine" that subtly alters platforming physics based on your play style — aggressive players encounter tighter, more frantic jumps, while explorers face longer, serene zero-G corridors. ## ✨ Core Features: Beyond the Standard Checklist

### 🧠 **Adaptive Gravity Engine (AGE)**
- Real-time physics recalibration based on player momentum, environmental hazards, and quest urgency.
- No two playthroughs share identical jump arcs or puzzle solutions.
- Visual telegraphs (rippling light) subtly hint at upcoming gravity shifts without breaking immersion.

### 🗣️ **Narrative Echo System**
- A multilingual dialogue system supporting **12 languages** with dynamic lip-sync and contextual tone shaping.
- Characters remember your choices and alter their body language, not just their text — a subtle nod to long-term consequence.

### 🛠️ **Modular Performance Profiles**
- Three preset tiers (Smooth Operator, Balanced Navigator, Ultra Dreamer) and a fully manual scaler.
- The game automatically benchmarks your hardware on first launch and suggests a stable baseline, with zero user intervention required.

### ☁️ **Synchronized Drift Saves**
- Cloud-based progression that merges your local save with server state without conflict resolution screens.
- Seamless cross-device continuation — start on a high-end desktop, finish on a modest laptop.

### 🎧 **Spatial Audio Intelligence**
- Every sound source, from distant metal groans to your own footsteps, is ray-traced for positional accuracy.
- A "Narrative Focus" mode quiets ambient noise during dialogue, but only when the player is still — motion restores the full soundscape.

### 🤝 **Community-Driven Hotfixes**
- Weekly telemetry reports are aggregated into public roadmaps.
- 24/7 Player Support Concierge that can inject non-invasive adjustments to your local settings file via an in-game chat command (text-based, no external tools).

## 📦 Getting Started With Your First Drift

Before you plunge into the neon-lit underbelly of *Aurelia*, here’s a conceptual overview of your initiation path. No command-line acrobatics required — we believe in a "launch and live" philosophy.

### System Requirements (Conceptual Benchmarks)
- **Minimum:** A quad-core processor, 8GB RAM, and a GPU with 4GB VRAM (DirectX 12 Ultimate capable).
- **Recommended:** A mid-range 6-core CPU, 16GB RAM, and a GPU offering 8GB VRAM.
- **Storage:** 30GB of SSD space (NVMe strongly advised for the zero-stutter promise).
- **Network:** A stable broadband connection is required for the cloud merge feature, but the core game is playable offline.

### Your First 15 Minutes
1. **Boot Sequence:** The game runs a 10-second hardware handshake, silently tuning shader caches and input buffers.
2. **Tutorial Integration:** Instead of text prompts, you learn the "Drift Throw" mechanic by mimicking a holographic companion's gesture in a mirrored space.
3. **Choice Fork:** You decide to repair the station's water recycler (altruistic path) or harvest its copper wiring (pragmatic path) — this opens divergent quest lines immediately.

> [!NOTE]
> **Beta Limitation:** The civilian AI population is capped at 80% of final density, and one of the three main hubs ("The Verdant Spire") is partially gated. This is intentional — we're stress-testing network sync under load during peak hours.

## 🗺️ The World of *Aurelia*: A Narrative Mosaic

*Aurelia* is a pre-Cataclysm orbital habitat that drifted off-course 200 years ago. You play as *Kaelen*, a "Rigger" — one of the few engineers capable of re-calibrating the Age Engine manually. The station is split into **three distinct biomes**:

- **The Halo Arc** (Zero-G residential rings): Hovering marketplaces, abandoned school gymnasiums, and a mysterious broadcast tower that only plays static.
- **The Stratum Core** (High-G industrial base): Forced gravity crushing you into the deck, creating tense close-quarters firefights and heavy, labored movement.
- **The Shell Garden** (Greenhouse exterior): Artificial sunlight, overgrown hydroponics, and fragile oxygen pockets that creep you out in a different way.

The main narrative arc involves "the Hollowing" — a phenomenon where sections of the station disappear for milliseconds, only to reappear with altered layouts. Your AGE unit is the only tool capable of "phasing" you through these glitches.

We are obsessed with **frame-time consistency** over raw FPS. Here’s what our internal lab measured against the same hardware across 100 hours of gameplay:

- **Frame Pacing:** 99th percentile stutter reduced by **72%** versus the closed alpha.
- **CPU Utilization:** Lowered single-thread bottlenecks by enabling "cooperative job stealing" across worker threads — a 14% gain in crowded scenes.

## 🛠️ Developer Notes: A Transparent Build

We're not hiding our current bug list. Here are the **Top 3 Known Issues** in this beta:

1. **Photon Mesh Flicker:** On specific AMD GPUs (RX 7900 series), certain light sources may pulse intermittently. Workaround: Toggle "Contact Shadows" off and on.
2. **Save Merge Conflict:** In rare Cloud Saves scenarios where two devices have played offline simultaneously, the merge logic may prioritize the older save. This is by design (safer rollback).
3. **Dialogue Subtlety:** The Narrative Echo System can sometimes shift tone too early during a character's line if you perform a quick traversal move. We're adjusting the attention-threshold calculation for the final release.
